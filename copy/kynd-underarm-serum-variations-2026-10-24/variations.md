## Compliance and claims checklist (check before launch)

- **Meta personal-attributes policy:** All text is first-person ("maine", "mera"). Don't change it to "*Aap ke* underarms dark hain?" ("*Your* underarms are dark?"). Calling out a viewer's physical traits gets ads rejected.
- **Wording:** Keep to "brighten / even tone / dark spots". Avoid "gora / fair / whitening".
- **Claims used are only ones already in the winning ad or on the product page:** 3 acids, visible results in 2 weeks (the site says this varies by skin tone), odour control, 1000+ customers, 100-day money-back guarantee. `[VERIFY]` usage directions (time of day, number of pumps) against the pack before shooting Body 1.
- **Offer:** The original shows a **12.12 sale / code DC20**. Don't reuse that site footage after the sale ends; re-record it with the current offer.
- **Comparisons:** "Regular deo / regular serum" and the lemon/potato remedies are generic. Show no competitor brand names or logos.
- **Testimonials / comment-reply sticker:** Use only real customer content you have permission to use.
