## Top 3 picks for testing first

1. **Variation 3 (Problem–Solution, ICP1, "Turn Your Day Into a Workout")** — the core brand promise ("no time for the gym") in its cleanest, most literal format. Lowest risk, highest likely relevance for cold time-starved-persona traffic.
2. **Variation 7 (Comparison Chart, ICP3, "Not Just Leggings")** — differentiates on tangible, verifiable specs (bands, wash care, returns) rather than soft stats — durable against ad fatigue and safe compliance-wise.
3. **Variation 5 (Benefit Stack, ICP3, "Built-In Resistance. Everyday Comfort.")** — shortest, cleanest benefit list with the money-back guarantee as risk reversal; good low-cost first test for warm/retargeting audiences before investing in longer-form creative.

Hold Variation 10 out of the first wave until the "up to 30%" study linkage is confirmed — test it only after that sign-off.
